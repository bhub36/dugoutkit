About DugoutKit

DugoutKit is a small family business built around one idea: the boring parts of a baseball season — the stat math, the tryout paperwork, the team budget — shouldn't take more time than the game itself.

These tools started as our own messy spreadsheets, kept through years of seasons, bullpens, and long drives home. We rebuilt them properly so any player, coach, or team parent can use them.

A few things we believe: spreadsheets you own beat apps you rent. A written-down number beats a remembered one. And a kid's trend line over twelve games matters more than any single Saturday.

When you email support, a real person from our family answers — usually the same day.
