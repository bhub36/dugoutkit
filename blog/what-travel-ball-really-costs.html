What Travel Ball Really Costs: a Parent's Honest Budget

DugoutKit Guides

Surveyed travel-ball families report $3,000–$8,000+ per season, and almost everyone underestimates going in, because the sticker price (team dues) is only the down payment.

The real line items

ItemTypical range
Team dues$800–$3,500
Hotels (6–10 travel weekends)$800–$1,800
Gas / flights$400–$1,500
Food on the road$300–$800
Equipment (bats age out, feet grow)$300–$800
Lessons (optional, common)$0–$1,500
Camps / showcases (older ages)$0–$800

Questions that save four figures

  1. "What exactly do dues cover?" Uniforms? Tournament fees? Insurance? "Extra tournaments billed separately" is where budgets die.
  2. "How many overnight weekends, realistically?" Ask last year's parents, not the coach.
  3. "What's the refund policy if we leave mid-season?" Get it in writing.
